\# StockWise

\### Inventory Replenishment \& Supplier Performance Analysis



\## 1. Project Overview



StockWise is a data-driven inventory management and analytics project designed to address common warehouse inventory challenges.



The project focuses on identifying stockout risks, detecting excess inventory, evaluating supplier performance, and supporting data-backed replenishment decisions.



The objective is to transform raw inventory and purchasing data into actionable business insights through SQL, Python, statistical analysis, and Power BI.



\## 2. Business Problem



Warehouse operations often face two major inventory challenges:



\- \*\*Stockouts:\*\* Insufficient inventory can disrupt operations and result in missed sales.

\- \*\*Overstocking:\*\* Excess inventory ties up working capital and increases storage costs.



Additionally, inconsistent supplier performance can delay replenishment and create inventory planning difficulties.



StockWise aims to analyze these problems and provide data-driven recommendations.



\## 3. Project Objectives



\- Identify products at risk of stockouts.

\- Detect excess and slow-moving inventory.

\- Analyze inventory levels and replenishment requirements.

\- Evaluate supplier reliability and performance.

\- Develop actionable purchasing and inventory recommendations.

\- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making.
